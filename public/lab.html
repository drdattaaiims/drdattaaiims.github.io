CRASH Lab

Centre for Responsible Autonomous Systems in Healthcare · clinician-led clinical AI evaluation at the Koita Centre for Digital Health, Ashoka University.

I founded and lead CRASH Lab to build benchmarks, datasets and evaluation frameworks for medical AI systems that must work safely in real clinical settings. The lab maintains its own authoritative website for current research, people, benchmark releases and opportunities.
