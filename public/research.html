Research & Work

I study what evidence clinical AI needs before it can influence care: not only whether a model is accurate, but whether it is reliable, safe, locally valid and able to hand over when uncertain.

Clinical AI evaluation and safety

Radiology's Last Exam (RadLE) tests frontier multimodal systems against radiologists on difficult clinical cases. I am co-first author of RadLE 1.0 and co-lead of RadLE 2.0. The second evaluation expands beyond accuracy to confidence, reliability, high-confidence errors and explicit deferral.

Multilingual and ambient clinical AI

PrAImaan and V2DD evaluate clinical AI systems in Indian workflows. My roles are Clinical Research and Technical Teams Supervisor for PrAImaan and Clinical Evaluation Team Supervisor for V2DD, under Principal Investigator Prof. Anurag Agrawal. The work includes blinded evaluation of healthcare AI and multilingual voice-to-structured-data systems, including omissions, hallucinations, correction burden, privacy, usability and interoperability.

LMIC validation and health equity

I lead an India–Singapore programme supported through the DST–A*STAR Joint Science and Technology Research Programme. It focuses on indigenous radiology datasets, subgroup evaluation and vision-language models intended for South Asian populations. The larger question is whether performance demonstrated on one population, institution or language survives contact with another.

Clinical decision support

PREDICT-AI is an IndiaAI CATCH-supported consortium with Tata Memorial Hospital and Manentia AI. As Principal Investigator, I work on clinical evaluation for decision support around post-chemotherapy retroperitoneal lymph-node dissection planning in testicular cancer.

Medical imaging and reporting systems

My imaging work spans AIRib, an AIIMS New Delhi–IIT Jodhpur pipeline for rib-fracture detection and characterisation; a MICCAI 2025 collaboration on fine-grained rib-fracture diagnosis with hyperbolic embeddings; and MedVersa, a generalist foundation model published in NEJM AI, for which I contributed to the radiologist evaluation workstream. Earlier reporting studies examined LLM-augmented speech-recognition pipelines and reliability in radiology tasks.
